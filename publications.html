Shallow corals acclimate to mesophotic depths while maintaining their heat tolerance against ongoing climate change

Tavakoli-Kolour P, Sinniger F, Morita M, Hazraty-Kari S, Nakamura T, Harii S

Marine Pollution Bulletin, 209, 117277

Research highlight
Shallow corals transplanted to 40 m depth retained their thermal tolerance and developed higher thermal thresholds. The study demonstrates how mesophotic environments can act as potential refugia for shallow corals under ocean warming.

2024

Susceptibility of Acropora tenuis to consecutive thermal stress

Hazraty-Kari S, Tavakoli-Kolour P, Nakamura T, Morita M

Coral Reefs, 43, 4

Research highlight
Repeated bleaching-level heat stress progressively reduced coral survival, photosynthetic efficiency, and symbiotic algal density. The results show that consecutive heatwaves can progressively increase coral susceptibility rather than producing unlimited acclimation.
