Selected research on coral ecophysiology, thermal stress, coral acclimation, mesophotic ecosystems and reef health.

2025 DIVERSITY

Can shading drive the transition from black band disease to a cyanobacterial patch–like stage in Montipora corals?

Das RR*, Tavakoli-Kolour P, Tang SL, Reimer JD (2025). Diversity, 17(10), 732.

Photographic observations document a possible transition from black band disease to a cyanobacterial patch-like stage in shaded Montipora fragments, motivating further study of light and disease dynamics.

2024 MARINE POLLUTION BULLETIN

Shallow corals acclimate to mesophotic depths while maintaining their heat tolerance against ongoing climate change

Tavakoli-Kolour P*, Sinniger F, Morita M, Hazraty-Kari S, Nakamura T, Harii S* (2024). Marine Pollution Bulletin, 209, 117277.

Tests thermal tolerance after two shallow-water coral species spent a year at 40 m depth. The transplanted corals retained high heat tolerance despite acclimation to cooler, low-light conditions.

2024 CORAL REEFS

Susceptibility of Acropora tenuis to consecutive thermal stress

Hazraty-Kari S*, Tavakoli-Kolour P, Nakamura T, Morita M* (2024). Coral Reefs, 43, 1097–1107.

Examines adult Acropora tenuis exposed to bleaching-level heat stress over three consecutive years, showing declining survival alongside changes in symbiont density and photosynthetic performance.
