RESEARCH

Research

Understanding coral resilience, environmental change and the future of tropical reefs.

RESEARCH PROGRAMME

From coral physiology to reef-scale change

My research investigates how corals respond to environmental variability across spatial, temporal and biological scales.

I combine field observations, long-term ecological monitoring and controlled experiments to understand the mechanisms underlying coral resilience, acclimation, bleaching and mortality.

RESEARCH THEME 01

Coral Reef Ecology

Coral reefs are dynamic ecosystems shaped by interactions between organisms and their physical environment.

My ecological research examines coral community structure, bleaching, mortality and changes in reef composition across environmental gradients and through time.

Key questions

  • Why do some coral colonies survive extreme environmental stress while others die?
  • How does coral size influence bleaching and mortality?
  • How do repeated disturbances reshape coral reef communities?

RESEARCH THEME 02

Coral Physiology & Thermal Stress

Rising ocean temperatures are among the most important threats facing coral reefs.

My experimental research investigates coral thermal tolerance, bleaching responses and physiological consequences of acute and chronic temperature stress.

Key questions

  • What determines coral thermal tolerance?
  • How do the intensity and duration of heat stress affect coral performance?
  • Can previous thermal exposure improve resistance to future heatwaves?

RESEARCH THEME 03

Depth & Mesophotic Coral Ecosystems

Deeper coral habitats experience different combinations of light, temperature and other environmental conditions.

I investigate whether corals can acclimate to deeper environments and whether mesophotic reefs can contribute to coral persistence under environmental change.

Key questions

  • Can shallow corals acclimate to mesophotic environments?
  • Does depth-related acclimation influence thermal tolerance?
  • Can deeper habitats function as refugia?

RESEARCH THEME 04

Coral Diseases & Reef Health

Coral health is influenced by interactions among temperature, environmental stressors, pathogens and host condition.

My work examines patterns of coral health, disease and mortality within environmentally challenging reef systems.

Key questions

  • How do environmental conditions influence coral health?
  • Which coral colonies are most vulnerable?
  • How do disease and thermal stress interact?

RESEARCH THEME 05

Environmental Change & Coral Resilience

Coral resilience depends not only on the magnitude of environmental stress, but also on its timing, variability and history.

I am particularly interested in environmental memory, thermal history and the limits of coral acclimation under repeated climate-driven disturbances.

Key questions

  • How long can coral thermal memory persist?
  • Does environmental variability increase resilience?
  • Are there limits to coral acclimation?

RESEARCH THEME 06

Field & Experimental Research

A central part of my research approach is the integration of natural field observations with controlled laboratory experiments.

Field surveys reveal how coral communities respond to real environmental conditions, while experiments allow specific mechanisms and causal relationships to be tested.

Research approaches

  • Coral reef field surveys
  • Long-term ecological monitoring
  • Thermal stress experiments
  • Coral transplantation experiments
  • Environmental monitoring
  • Quantitative ecological modelling

RESEARCH APPROACH

Connecting experiments with ecological reality

The goal of my research is to move beyond describing patterns of coral decline and identify the mechanisms that determine coral persistence, resilience and vulnerability.
