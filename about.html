About me

BIOGRAPHY

Researching coral resilience across changing environments

I am a coral reef ecologist interested in understanding how corals respond to environmental variability and climate-driven change.

My research combines coral ecophysiology, reef ecology, experimental approaches and field-based observations to investigate coral bleaching, thermal tolerance, acclimation, adaptation and reef resilience.

I have worked across contrasting coral reef environments, including the Persian Gulf, Okinawa and tropical Southeast Asia. These systems provide opportunities to investigate how corals respond to extreme temperatures, depth-related environmental gradients and changing reef conditions.

My current research continues to integrate experimental and ecological approaches to understand the mechanisms that determine coral persistence under environmental change.
